# Portfolio Analysis Report — Procurement Cost Optimization

## Purpose
Compare suppliers and items across purchase price, lead time, and defect rates.

## Recommended report sections
- Executive overview: purchase volume and spend proxy.
- Supplier scorecard: price, lead time, defects, and sample size.
- Item comparison: supplier differences within the same item.
- Decision support: shortlist suppliers for quote validation.

## Interpretation guardrails
The data and benchmarks are synthetic. The current script summarizes supplier/item metrics; it is not a complete constrained optimization model and does not prove realized savings. Validate recommendations against current quotes, contracts, volume, and service requirements.

## Suggested action
Use the scorecard to prioritize negotiations, then test scenarios with explicit constraints and actual purchase-order data.
