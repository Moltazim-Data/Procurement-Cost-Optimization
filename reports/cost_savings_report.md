# Procurement Cost Optimization — Illustrative Report

> **Data note:** This report uses synthetic demonstration data. It does not represent actual supplier quotes or realized savings.

## Objective
Compare historical purchase prices with illustrative benchmark prices and identify items for supplier review.

## Method
- Aggregate purchase history by item and supplier.
- Compare current unit prices with benchmark prices.
- Rank candidate supplier/item combinations by estimated percentage savings.
- Validate recommendations against current quotes, lead times, quality requirements, and contract terms before purchasing.

## Interpretation
The CSV files in `dashboards/` contain example recommendations and benchmark comparisons. Savings are estimates from synthetic prices, not guaranteed or realized savings.
